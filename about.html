About Twin Fortune

Twin Fortune Ltd. is an indigenous procurement and supply company serving businesses and institutions.

We provide procurement solutions across industrial equipment, engineering and maintenance components, electrical and automation products, lubricants, safety supplies, technology, workplace supplies and other operational requirements.

Our work covers routine procurement, project-specific requirements and specialised items. Depending on the requirement, we source locally or internationally and coordinate the procurement and associated logistics through to delivery.
